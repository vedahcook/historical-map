How this map was made

Each year shows the borders in force on July 1. The map uses OpenHistoricalMap (OHM), which matched the historical record in 149 of 160 spot checks. Where OHM has no record, it falls back to CShapes-Europe (from 1816) or Cliopatria. Where our checks found OHM wrong, the map applies a correction and says so.

Alternative borders compare OHM with CShapes-Europe from 1816 and with Cliopatria before that. They count only when a source puts an area of at least 400 km² (5,000 km² for the coarser Cliopatria) in a different country for longer than a year. Territories that governed themselves under an overlord count as part of the overlord for this comparison, because the sources disagree on whether to show them.

Cities appear when a country is highlighted: about 250 of Europe's largest cities by 1900, placed in whichever country held them that year. Tap one for its population. Figures come from censuses where there is one for that year; between figures the map estimates, assuming steady growth, and says so. A hollow dot means there is no figure within ten years. Some early figures are estimates for the wider city, so they are not always comparable with later census counts.
